privacy policy●

last updated: September 29, 2026

rollcall! ("the app", "we") is made by David Myat. this policy explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. questions: thombie326@gmail.com.

what we collect

  • account: your email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash) if you sign up with email. if you use Google sign-in, Google shares your Google account ID, email, name and profile picture link. if you use Apple sign-in, Apple shares your Apple account ID, your email (which may be a private relay address) and, the first time only, your name.
  • profile: the display name you choose and a profile photo, if you add one.
  • game content: the trips you create or join, prompts you write, photos you post, your votes, pics you flag, and people you block.
  • trip timezone: when a trip admin starts a trip, the app reads the phone's timezone setting so daily rounds run on local time. it is not your location.
  • technical data: when you sign in, our backend provider records your IP address and device/app type (user agent) with your login session, and keeps request logs that include IP addresses.
  • notifications: if you allow them, your phone's push token, so we can send trip pings to this phone. turn pings off anytime on the you tab.

what we don't collect

  • no location. photos are re-encoded before upload, which removes the hidden photo data (EXIF), including GPS location and camera details.
  • no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • we don't sell your data.

how we use it

only to run the game: signing you in, showing your trips, photos and results to the people in the same trip, sending password reset codes, and handling reports so we can remove content that breaks our terms.

who can see your content

photos, prompts, votes and results are visible only to members of the same trip. photos are stored privately and shown through links that expire. anyone with a trip's room code can join that trip, so share codes only with people you trust. if you flag a pic, only we see that you flagged it.

services that handle your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, photo storage and server functions (United States).
  • Resend sends password reset emails and report emails. a report email to us includes the trip name, prompt text, the names and user IDs of the poster and the person who flagged it, and a link to the photo that expires after 7 days.
  • Expo delivers notifications, through Apple's and Google's push services. it receives your phone's push token and each notification's text, which includes the trip name.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.

these providers process data on our behalf to run the app.

on your phone

the app keeps your login and a cache of photos you've viewed (up to about 250 MB) on your device. the cache is cleared when you log out.

deleting your data

open you → privacy, help & delete → delete my account. this permanently removes your account, your sign-in details, your trip memberships and display names, your photos and their files, your votes, flags and blocks, other people's votes and flags on your photos, and any trips you created that nobody else joined.

some things remain:

  • prompts you wrote stay in their trips, with your name removed.
  • trips you created that others joined stay for them, and another member becomes admin.
  • report emails already sent to us (they expire from our inbox when we delete them; the photo link stops working after 7 days).
  • our providers' logs, which include IP addresses, until they expire under the providers' retention periods.
  • other members' phones may keep a cached copy of your photos until their app clears it.

to ask about or request deletion of anything else, email us.

kids

rollcall! is not meant for children under 13. we don't knowingly collect data from them. if you think a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

changes

if this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
