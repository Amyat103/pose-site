pose● support

pose is a photo game for trips with friends: daily prompts, everyone snaps a pic, the group votes.

questions, problems or a report?
email thombie326@gmail.com. we aim to reply within 24 hours.

report a pic

in a vote, press and hold the pic, then tap flag. it comes straight to us, and nobody in the trip sees who flagged it. we review reports and remove content that breaks our terms, usually within 24 hours. to report anything else (a prompt, a name, a person), email us.

block someone

in a vote, press and hold their pic, then tap block. you won't see their pics anywhere in the app, and they won't be told. to unblock, press and hold a hidden tile.

delete your account

open the you tab and tap delete my account. see the privacy policy for exactly what gets removed.

forgot your password

on the log in screen, tap forgot it?. we'll email you a 6-digit code.

pose is a working name and may change.
