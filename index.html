what is rollcall!

a photo game for trips with friends: daily prompts, everyone snaps a pic, the group votes.

start a trip, share the room code with your crew, and vote on each day's best pics. when the trip ends, you get a recap of every winner.

coming soon to iOS and Android

report a pic

press and hold any pic, then tap flag. it comes straight to us, and nobody in the trip sees who flagged it.

we remove anything that breaks our terms, usually within 24 hours. to report a prompt, a name or a person, email us.

block someone

press and hold any of their pics, then tap block. you won't see their pics anywhere in the app, and they won't be told.

to unblock, press and hold a hidden tile.

delete your account

you tab→privacy, help & delete→delete my account

can't open the app? email thombie326@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

deleted

your account and sign-in details, your profile name and photo, the photos you posted, your votes, flags and blocks, and any trips only you were in.

kept

prompts you wrote stay in their trips without your name; shared trips stay for the other members; server logs expire on their own. full details in the privacy policy.
